Publication

My publication reflects my long-standing interest in educational AI, especially how these systems should be designed and deployed responsibly.

Journal publication

Ethical Considerations for Companies Implementing LLMs in Education Software

International Journal of Innovative Science and Research Technology, 2024.
DOI: 10.38124/ijisrt/IJISRT24AUG1297

This single-author paper examines the responsible use of large language models in educational software and the risks companies should account for when deploying them to learners.

Open paper
Brief summary

What the paper is about

Core questionWhat should companies consider before integrating LLMs into educational products?
Main issuesPrivacy, misinformation, bias, over-reliance on AI, human oversight, and equitable access.
Why it mattersIt connects directly to my broader vision of educational AI that is effective, responsible, and human-centered.
